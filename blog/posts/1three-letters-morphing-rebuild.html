Three Letters, One Heart

Watch how the words transform while the truth stays constant

📅 February 2026 ⏱️ 25 min read 🏷️ Grief, Evolution

This is the same letter, written three times.

Most of the text stayed the same across all three versions. Those are the truths that didn't need to change.

What changed is where the growth lives.

How This Works

The text you're reading is the final version (Phase III). But some sentences were different in earlier drafts — written when I was angrier, more desperate, less accepting.

Click "Start Morphing" below to watch those specific sentences transform. The stable text stays still. Only the parts that evolved will flicker between versions.

It's chaotic. It's unsettling. It's exactly how grief feels.
