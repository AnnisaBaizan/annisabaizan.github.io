Key Features

Complete lifecycle management for laboratory equipment from acquisition through scheduled maintenance and repair.

📋
Equipment Registry
Centralized database of all lab equipment with specifications, acquisition dates, warranty information, and current condition status.
📅
Maintenance Scheduling
Automated maintenance schedule generation based on equipment type and usage frequency, with calendar reminders for due dates.
🔧
Repair Request Workflow
Structured repair request submission and tracking — from initial report through technician assignment to resolution and sign-off.
📜
History Log
Complete maintenance and repair history per equipment item, providing full audit trail for accreditation and compliance needs.
❤️
Condition Reports
Equipment health assessments with condition ratings. Automatically flag items that need attention before critical failures occur.
📊
Maintenance Dashboard
Overview of upcoming maintenance, overdue items, open repair requests, and equipment condition distribution at a glance.
