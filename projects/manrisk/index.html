Risk Management System

MANRISK

An organizational risk management system for Poltekkes Kemenkes Palembang — enabling institutional units to identify, assess, and mitigate risks with a visual heat map and structured monitoring workflow.

Risk Calculation Model

MANRISK uses a standard institutional risk formula to quantify and categorize organizational risks.

⏳ This project is currently in the pending development queue. Architecture and specifications are finalized.
Risk Formula
  • TR = Kemungkinan × Dampak
  • TR = Probability × Impact
  • Scale: 1–5 for each dimension
  • Maximum TR = 25
  • 5-level classification output
Risk Levels
  • Very Low — TR 1–4
  • Low — TR 5–9
  • Medium — TR 10–14
  • High — TR 15–19
  • Very High — TR 20–25
User Roles
  • Admin — full system access
  • Unit User — own unit only
  • Guest — read-only view
  • Per-unit data scoping
  • Audit trail for changes

Key Features

Comprehensive risk management from identification through monitoring and reporting.

🔍
Risk Identification
Record and map organizational risks per unit — categorizing by type, source, and affected processes with structured data entry.
📋
Activity Management
Manage risk mitigation programs and activities — linking each action to specific identified risks and responsible parties.
📊
Monitoring & Evaluation
Track mitigation progress with three-state status: In Progress, Completed, or Not Done. Escalation alerts for overdue items.
📁
Reporting
Upload and manage RKT (annual work plans) and quarterly progress reports. Archive and retrieve historical risk reports by period.
🗺️
Risk Heat Map
Visual 5×5 probability-impact heat map showing risk distribution across all organizational units at a glance.
🔐
Role-Based Access
Three-tier access: Admin sees all units; Unit Users manage their own unit's risks; Guests have read-only institutional overview.
