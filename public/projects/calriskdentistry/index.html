Clinical Decision Support System

CalRiskDentistry

A clinical decision support system that predicts dental calculus and caries risk through evidence-based weighted scoring algorithms — empowering clinicians with data-driven risk stratification at the point of care.

Two Clinical Modules

CalRiskDentistry combines two complementary risk prediction modules under one clinical decision support framework.

CalRiskDentistry Module
  • Calculus (tartar) risk prediction
  • 7-factor weighted scoring algorithm
  • Oral hygiene habits assessment
  • Diet & medical history factors
  • Clinical examination findings
  • Low / Medium / High stratification
CavityCare Module
  • Dental caries (cavity) risk concept
  • Evidence-based scoring rubric
  • Knowledge base of risk indicators
  • Clinical factor weighting
  • Patient-level risk profiling
  • Preventive care recommendations
Risk Stratification
  • Score 0–33: Low Risk
  • Score 34–66: Medium Risk
  • Score 67–100: High Risk
  • Weighted clinical indicators
  • Evidence-based thresholds
  • Actionable clinical output

System Capabilities

Designed to support clinical decisions with transparent, reproducible scoring logic.

🧮
Weighted Scoring Algorithm
Seven clinical factors each contribute a weighted score. Transparent algorithm with evidence-based factor weights derived from literature.
📚
Clinical Knowledge Base
Structured knowledge base encoding clinical expertise — oral hygiene habits, diet patterns, medical history, and examination findings.
⚡
Risk Stratification Engine
Automatically classifies patients into Low, Medium, or High risk categories based on cumulative weighted score.
🔬
Evidence-Based Design
All scoring parameters derived from peer-reviewed dental research and clinical practice guidelines for calculus and caries prevention.
📊
Patient Risk Profiling
Generate individual patient risk profiles showing contribution of each clinical factor to the overall risk score.
💡
Clinical Recommendations
Risk output paired with tailored preventive care recommendations for dentists to communicate to patients.

Technology & Methods

Research-grade implementation combining algorithmic scoring with a structured clinical knowledge base.
